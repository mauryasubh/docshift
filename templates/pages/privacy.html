Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

We operate on a zero-tracking philosophy. We do not gather, index, or analyze personal user information. When you upload a file, we temporarily collect and process it solely for the purpose of document conversion.

2. Document Storage and Auto-Deletion

All uploaded files and converted outputs are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers:

  • Within 1 Hour for anonymous guest sessions.
  • Within 24 Hours for logged-in registered accounts.

No backup archives or permanent copies of your files are ever maintained. Once deleted, files are completely unrecoverable.

3. Cookies & Local Data

ShiftDocs uses only essential session cookies to remember your conversion history and keep you logged in. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or behavioral targeting scripts. Your data stays entirely in your browser and on your dashboard.

4. Security Measures

We employ rigorous technical security measures to safeguard document workflows. All data transfers between your browser and our servers are encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.3). In addition, servers run automated sandboxed processing to prevent internal access.

5. Subprocessors

We partner with secure, industry-leading infrastructure and payment providers to host our application and process billing:

  • Razorpay: Secure Payment Gateway (UPI, Cards, Netbanking)
  • AWS (Amazon Web Services): Secure Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure
