Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about file security, size limits, subscriptions, or API usage? We have got you covered.

Your document privacy is our absolute priority.

  • Guest Uploads: Files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers 1 hour after conversion.
  • Registered Users: Files are saved in your dashboard and kept for 24 hours before deletion.

All document transfers are secured with bank-grade SSL/TLS encryption, and we never read, archive, inspect, or share your document contents.

Our file limits are tier-dependent:

  • Free/Guest Tier: Up to 10MB per file.
  • Developer Tier: Up to 50MB per file, with 5,000 monthly API calls.
  • Corporate Tier: Up to 500MB+ per file with high-priority processing queues.

We do not store your card details on our servers; all payments are processed directly by our secure payment gateway providers.
