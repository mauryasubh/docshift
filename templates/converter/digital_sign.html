Why I Built This
I looked everywhere for a free tool to digitally sign my PDFs — not just slap an image on them, but actually cryptographically sign them so no one can tamper with the content. Every tool I found either charged money, required you to hand over your documents, or didn't offer verification.

ShiftDocs signs your documents for free, stores nothing, and lets anyone verify if a document has been changed. No other free tool does all three. Your document never touches any disk — it's processed in memory and gone the moment you download it.
